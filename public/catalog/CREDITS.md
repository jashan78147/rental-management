# Photography

Seven photographs, used as backdrops for the hero and the six category cards.
Everything else on the site is drawn (`components/gear-art.tsx`), because a
photo of the wrong camera body on a line named after a specific one is worse
than no photo at all.

All from **Pexels**, under the [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/):
free for commercial use, no attribution required. Recorded here anyway, so the
source of every file in the repo is known.
